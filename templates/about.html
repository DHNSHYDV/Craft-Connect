About Desh Ke Haath

India is home to one of the richest and most diverse cultural heritages in the world. From handwoven textiles to intricate metalwork, every region carries traditions shaped by centuries of skill, devotion, and identity. Yet much of this craftsmanship remains unseen beyond local communities.

Behind every craft is an artisan — someone who has inherited knowledge passed down through generations. Many of these artisans face limited visibility, restricted market access, and dependence on intermediaries, making it difficult for their work to reach national and international audiences. As a result, their skills often go unrecognized, and traditional art forms struggle to gain the appreciation they deserve.

Desh Ke Haath was created to change that.

We believe Indian handicrafts are not just products — they are stories, history, and living traditions. Our platform brings together crafts from across all Indian States and Union Territories into one space, making them easier to discover, understand, and value.

Through thoughtful use of technology and AI, Desh Ke Haath helps people explore the cultural significance behind each craft while providing artisans with direct visibility — without unnecessary middlemen.

This is more than a platform.

It is a step toward preserving heritage, empowering artisans, and strengthening the spirit of Vocal for Local and Atmanirbhar Bharat.

Because when Indian hands create, India grows stronger.
