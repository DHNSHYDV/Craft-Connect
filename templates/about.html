About Desh Ke Haath

The Heritage Gap
Desh Ke Haath was created with a simple thought — India has a rich heritage and beautiful handicrafts, but many people never truly get to know them. When people travel, they experience places and cultures, yet often return without something that carries the soul of that place, mainly because they are unaware of local crafts and artisans.
The Artisan Challenge
On the other hand, many artisans work with great skill and tradition, but struggle to reach wider audiences. Their crafts remain limited to small regions, and the stories, history, and effort behind their work often go unseen. This gap between creators and people leads to traditional art forms slowly losing visibility.
Our Solution
Desh Ke Haath brings Indian handicrafts from across the country onto one platform, along with their cultural background and stories. Instead of acting as a middleman, the app focuses on giving artisans and their crafts direct visibility, helping people understand and value the tradition behind every product.
Technology as a Bridge
Through Desh Ke Haath, technology becomes a bridge — connecting people to India's heritage and helping traditional crafts stay alive, appreciated, and remembered.
